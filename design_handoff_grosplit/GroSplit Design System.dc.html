08
Iconography, motion & voice
Iconography
Outline only, 1.75px stroke, 24px box, round caps and joins. Geometric primitives — square, circle, chevron, plus, check. No filled glyphs, no duotone, never an icon where a two-word label is clearer. Money never gets an icon; the $ is the icon.
+
✓
Motion
Screen push 280ms cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1). Sheets slide 320ms. Row taps 120ms scale to .98. Numbers count up over 600ms when a split recalculates — that tick is the app's signature. Poll votes land with an 8px slide-in, no bounce. Nothing loops, nothing pulses.
Voice
Housemate, not bank. Short, warm, a little dry. “Kai's out on the protein powder.” Never “transaction”, “settlement” or “participant”. Amounts are always stated plainly and never softened — the whole point is that nobody has to ask twice.

Rules of the paper
Receipt surfaces are square-cornered with torn top and bottom edges, never a rounded card. Store names are mono, uppercase, .14em tracked and centered. Line items are mono uppercase with a dotted leader running to a right-aligned price. Group breaks are dashed; every total sits between a dashed rule above and a double rule below. Excluded lines keep their price but go ink-40 and struck through — the paper never forgets an item, it just stops charging for it.
Where paper stops
Anything the user touches is app chrome, not paper: buttons, inputs, sheets, chips and the tab bar keep 12–14px radii and Space Grotesk. So a screen reads as a printed slip with soft controls resting on top — one texture for facts, one for actions.
Never fake thermal-print artifacts — no faded rows, no skewed rotation, no barcode graphics. The receipt is a layout metaphor, not a costume.
